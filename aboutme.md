My name is Daniel Deandresi and came to the United States from Argentina in 2001. I describe myself as a self-taught and starter individual. I have built my own cybersecurity homebrew lab to ramp-up my career prior to graduating as a Valedictorian in Systems and Cybersecurity. 

I possess 10 years working experience as a Penetration Tester and Offensive Security Engineer. I love to indulge into creative ways to find flaws in any system. When away from work, I love solving labs in TryHackMe and HackTheBox, CTFs, and hone my recon methodology and Bug Bounty skills in which I plan to improve over the following years.

I really enjoy mentoring and teaching people of all levels and backgrounds. If you would like assistance getting into the cybersecurity scene, please contact me. I will gladly help you share my knowledge as long you are willing to work for it and are not just looking to be spoon-fed.
