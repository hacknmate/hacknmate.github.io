My name is Daniel Deandresi and came to the United States from Argentina in 2001. I describe myself as a self-taught and starter individual. I have built my own cybersecurity homebrew lab to ramp-up my career prior to graduating as a Valedictorian in Cybersecurity. I am a meritocratic who believes in chasing opportunities instead of waiting for them.

I have been a Penetration tester and consultant for 10 years with experience across diverse verticals. Driven by the belief that no system is 100% secure, I specialize in finding creative flaws in non-conventional ways. Outside of work, I actively hone my skills through CTFs, Labs (TryHackMe/HackTheBox), Bug Bounties, Chess and practice Italian.

I really enjoy mentoring and teaching people of all levels and backgrounds. If you would like assistance getting into the cybersecurity scene, please contact me. I will gladly help you share my knowledge as long you are willing to work for it and are not just looking to be spoon-fed.
